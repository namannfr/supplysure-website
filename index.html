The problem

Approved isn’t the same as ready.

A supplier can hold every accreditation and still fail the checks for one specific job. Today, that gap is found too late.

Today

  1. Approved
  2. No job-specific check
  3. Bid effort spent
  4. Gap found at mobilisation

With SupplySure

  1. Approved
  2. Gap found and fixed
  3. Bid with confidence
  4. Ready on day one

Approval is general

A certificate proves a standard at one point in time, not fit for this package, project and date.

Evidence is scattered

Portals, spreadsheets, shared drives and email. Checked again on every project.

Gaps surface late

Often after the bid is written. A typical SME tender costs £500–£4,000 to prepare.1
